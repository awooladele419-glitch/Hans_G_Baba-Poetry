“Sheyi Awo.”

Written in tribute by Adefisayo Adesoye on your 2014 Birthday

Friend

This friend, that I may not have appreciated... tho' to me 1 in a million.

When I see my friend, there's this question that usually pops up inside me.. 'am I a true friend?'...

Not bcos i do evil, but bcos He'll do 4 me wht am nt quite sure I could do 4 Him.

He's very special....making everybody laugh regardless; wht d persons' condition is.

His ability 2 adapt 2 any condition... I even envy.

My friend, True friend is a year older 2day.

Happy birthday mr. Awoniran, I wish you long live and prosperity.

Preserved from Awóníran Olúwaséyí’s March 16, 2014 timeline archive — standing right beside “RESPONSIBILITIES!!! (HBD TO ME)” as a testament to enduring brotherhood and mutual respect during the FUTA years.
